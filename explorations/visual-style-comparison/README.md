# Practical AI visual comparison

Two 1536 × 1024 visuals for the same website story: a business team starts with a real task, tries one AI-supported step and reviews the result.

- `generative-team-workflow.png` — generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. It is an editorial illustration, not a photograph of Practical AI staff or a client session.
- `scripted-team-workflow.svg` — generated entirely from SVG primitives and editable text by `build_svg.py`. Regenerate with `python3 build_svg.py`.
- `index.html` — local, responsive comparison board with side-by-side and single-image views.

## Image generation prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial visual for the Practical AI corporate training website, to compare against a scripted SVG alternative. Scene: four adult business colleagues from varied backgrounds gathered around a table, collaboratively reviewing one practical AI workflow on a laptop and a few printed process cards. The screen shows only abstract blocks and connectors, with no legible text. The group is engaged in a concrete working session, not posing for a stock photo. Medium: premium natural editorial photography, subtle film grain, believable anatomy and hands, restrained art direction. Composition: wide landscape 3:2, faces and hands visible, enough clear space around subjects for a responsive website crop. Lighting: warm natural window light with soft shadows. Palette: deep navy, off-white, muted blue gray, restrained yellow accent aligned with Practical AI branding. Constraints: no logo, no words, no numbers, no watermarks, no floating holograms, no glowing AI brain, no exaggerated futuristic UI. Make the scene visually distinct from the current generic office group photo; documentary realism and purposeful interaction.

## Decision

Use generated scenes for editorial atmosphere and program introductions. Use scripted SVG for the method and workflow explanations, especially when text, localization and responsive sharpness matter. Case evidence should lead with sourced figures, attributed quotes and real client materials. Synthetic scenes must remain clearly labelled as illustrations.
