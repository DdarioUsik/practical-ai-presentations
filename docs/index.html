A bounded first decision

AI Opportunity & Readiness Assessment

€5,0002–3 weeks

A focused assessment of selected functions and processes. The interview sample and process depth are agreed with your sponsor before work begins.

What the assessment includes

  • Interviews across up to eight agreed functions, with selected leaders and employees
  • A short employee survey and review of selected recurring processes
  • IT, Security and governance constraints for the selected workflows
  • Screening of 10–15 candidate initiatives and prioritisation of 3–5
  • Opportunity Map and Readiness & Risk assessment
  • Preliminary business case with explicit assumptions
  • Proposed 12-month roadmap and executive decision session

Client inputs

  • An executive sponsor and process owners
  • Interview access and available process information
  • IT and Security participation on tools, data and feasibility
  • Timely feedback on assumptions and findings

The fee covers the assessment. Implementation, training, integrations and later phases receive a separate scope and agreement. The company decides whether to continue after the assessment.

Questions leaders ask

What to expect from the first step.

Is this a training programme?+

The assessment starts with business processes, readiness and constraints. Its findings can point to training, a workflow pilot, technical discovery, governance work or another next step.

Do we need to choose AI tools first?+

The assessment records your current stack and IT and Security boundaries. Tool choices follow the process, risk and feasibility review.

What happens after 2–3 weeks?+

You receive the assessment outputs and a decision session. Your company can continue internally or agree a separate next phase with Practical AI.

Can you guarantee savings or ROI?+

The work establishes baselines, tests assumptions and records observed changes. Financial outcomes follow actual adoption, costs and measured use of released capacity.

Who owns security and compliance decisions?+

Your authorised IT, Security, Legal and risk owners set and approve the applicable boundaries. Practical AI documents those decisions for the selected workflows.

Can our current AI programmes continue?+

Yes. Company labs, leadership sprints and role-based programmes remain available as focused formats and supporting pages.
