Frequently asked questions

Start with your team’s work.

Who is the program for?

Team leads, HR and L&D, and people in product, sales, finance and operations. Participants bring tasks they know well.

Which tools will the team use?

The choice depends on your company’s tools and policies. Before practice, we agree on suitable tools, access and sample materials.

What does the team take away?

Practical workflows, review criteria, a team practice library and a plan for the next step.

How do we choose between the one-week sprint and four-week program?

Choose the sprint to test one workflow with a focused team. Choose the four-week program to build AI practice across several roles with guided follow-up.

Start with one task

Which workflow would your team like to improve with AI?

Tell us about the team, a repeatable task and the number of people involved. We can map out a suitable program together.
