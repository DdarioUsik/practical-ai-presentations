How Practical AI works

A team approach to practical AI learning.

We bring learning, business and AI practice together around your team's work. The task defines the format and the mix of expertise involved.

One shared method

We start with a recurring task and the people responsible for it. Together we map an AI step, practise on a representative example, review the output and document a next test for normal work.

A team around the task

Program roles follow the team's needs, tools and workflow. Practical AI coordinates the learning design, working sessions and review so each group has a clear path from its first task to a practical next step.
