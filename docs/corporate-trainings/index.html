Your 12-month roadmap

Four quarters. One corporate team. A growing portfolio of AI solutions.

Q1Weeks 1–13
Focus · Discover & build

Build your first AI assistants

We start with your team's real work: recurring tasks, bottlenecks, and processes where AI makes a measurable difference.

  • Assess AI readiness across up to 300 employees and 4–6 business functions through interviews, a skills survey and process mapping.
  • Identify and prioritize 30+ potential use cases on a value × feasibility × adoption matrix.
  • Select 3–5 pilot projects with an economic model for each: volume, cost per task, expected savings.
  • Train a first cohort of 20–30 employees in a 4-module hands-on course: role-based prompting, research and competitor analysis, knowledge work with NotebookLM and Perplexity, and rapid prototyping with Claude, Gamma and Lovable.
  • Build and test 5 AI assistants on real business tasks, during the sessions, with your data.

How we teach

Five principles to take you from theory to application.

Fast and sustainable. Your team learns by doing, on its own tasks, and leaves every session with something that works.

  1. 01

    Hands-on first

    Instead of just listening to theory, your team works with us in practice from the very first session. Through our challenges, people apply AI to their own tasks right away and gain real experience.

  2. 02

    Community

    No one learns alone. In our learning environment, colleagues meet people with similar goals, share what works and learn from each other. Internal AI champions keep that exchange going.

  3. 03

    Experts

    Our trainers are practitioners who build AI systems for clients. They guide your team step by step, give feedback on real work and are always there for questions.

  4. 04

    Materials

    Easy-to-follow videos combined with concrete methods, clear checklists, role-based prompt libraries and proven frameworks from real projects.

  5. 05

    Flexibility

    On the go or between tasks. Short units and our practical learning app let employees fit the program into a normal working day.
