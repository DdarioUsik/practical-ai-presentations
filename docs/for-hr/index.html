For HR and learning leaders

What should the first program solve?

A useful brief starts with the work, not the workshop length.

Who is learning?Name the team, roles and process owner.
What work will they bring?Choose a recurring task with a visible output.
How will they check it?Agree on tools, data boundaries and human review.
What happens afterward?Define a small test for the next working week.
